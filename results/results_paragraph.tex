On the pooled random split evaluation, the best-performing classifier was the SVM model, which achieved an accuracy of 0.7957, a precision of 0.2480, a recall of 0.5196, and an F1 score of 0.3357. The LDA baseline yielded 0.7346 accuracy and 0.3119 F1, whereas the RBF-SVM reached 0.7957 accuracy and 0.3357 F1, indicating that nonlinear decision boundaries provided a measurable benefit for discriminating target from non-target RSVP responses. Across the full processed feature set, the experiment included 2041 target epochs and 18493 non-target epochs, demonstrating that the proposed ERP-window pipeline can support lightweight attention detection from only eight posterior EEG channels while remaining computationally practical for near-real-time inference.
